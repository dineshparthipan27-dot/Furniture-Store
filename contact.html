Common Inquiries

Do you offer international shipping and white-glove delivery?

Yes, we offer premium white-glove delivery services worldwide. Our dedicated logistics team ensures your bespoke pieces are carefully transported, unpacked, and assembled in your exact desired location. Shipping costs and timelines vary based on the destination.

Can I customize the fabric, leather, or wood finish on a piece?

Absolutely. Aura Living is rooted in bespoke craftsmanship. We offer an extensive library of premium fabrics, full-grain leathers, and ethically sourced wood finishes. Our design consultants will guide you through the selection process to match your vision perfectly.

What is the typical lead time for bespoke furniture?

Since every piece is handcrafted to order by our master artisans, our standard lead time is between 8 to 12 weeks. Intricate customizations or rare materials may extend this timeframe slightly. We provide regular progress updates throughout the crafting process.

How do I care for and maintain my luxury solid wood furniture?

We recommend dusting regularly with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid placing solid wood pieces in direct sunlight or near heat sources to prevent fading and cracking. A detailed care guide and a complimentary maintenance kit are included with every Aura Living purchase.
